uf_unleash_detect_resume spec_review false-negative (confirmed by instrumented reproduction 2026-10-31): defaultGitExec (opencode lib/uf-workflow-types.ts) returns execFileSync(...).trim(), which strips the LEADING SPACE of `git status --porcelain` XY-status prefixes on unstaged modifications (" M path" → "M path"). porcelainEntryPath then does line.slice(3) — calibrated for the untrimmed "XY path" format — producing a corrupted path ("penspec/changes/…" instead of "openspec/changes/…") that can never match specArtifactsDirty's excluded tasks.md path. Net effect: any UNSTAGED modification in the feature dir (typically tasks.md checkbox edits left by an in-flight implement phase) makes specArtifactsDirty report dirty, so the specs-mode freshness guard fails and detect_resume reports spec_review:false even when an approving dispatch artifact exists at the current HEAD. Staged entries ("M  path") and untracked ("?? path") are unaffected — only leading-space XY statuses (worktree-only modifications) are corrupted. Consequences: resumed /uf.unleash children burn their whole 30-min window re-deriving or re-running a redundant spec review (observed in delegations 6 and 7 of the #306 pipeline). Workarounds: (1) the uf.unleash checkpoint's completed_steps ("spec-review") plus orchestrator delegation prompts that pre-answer the false-negative and instruct the child to skip re-review and proceed to implement; (2) committing/staging the tasks.md checkbox edits makes the porcelain line leading-space-free and the probe pass naturally. Proper fix: porcelain consumers must not go through a trimming GitExec — parse porcelain output with a non-trimming exec or trim-tolerant parsing (e.g. match /^.. \s*/ or slice from the last status char). The code_review derivation (strict headSha equality, no dirty check) is NOT affected by this bug, but shares mostRecentArtifact; the implement probe (checkbox counting) is unaffected. Verified on gaze's vendored uf v0.21.0 (.opencode/plugins/uf-workflow/index.ts L2628-2636 + lib/uf-workflow-types.ts L60-61).
